The Pomodoro Technique

  • 1
    Choose a task you want to complete and select your subject.
  • 2
    Work intensely for 25 minutes until the chime rings. Avoid distractions.
  • 3
    Take a 5-minute break. Stretch, hydrate, or relax.
  • 4
    After 4 focus cycles, enjoy a longer 15-30 minute break.

Ensure your audio is enabled to hear the synthesized chime when your focus block completes!
